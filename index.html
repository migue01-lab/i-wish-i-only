Wishes & Regrets

Expressing emotions using I wish and If only

1

Meet Sofia

Understanding emotions and hidden desires

Sofia recently moved to a bustling, noisy city for university. Leaving her close-knit family and quiet hometown behind was harder than she expected. Everything feels fast-paced and overwhelming. Looking out her apartment window at the glittering skyline, a wave of homesickness washes over her.

"I wish I had my family with me right now."

Reflect on Sofia's Emotions

1. Current Reality: Is Sofia's family here with her right now?

2. The Emotion: How does Sofia feel about her current situation?

3. The Wish: What is she imagining?

I Wish vs. If Only

Practice structures for desires and regrets

To express a desire for a different reality now: Use I wish / If only + simple past.

To express a regret about the past: Use I wish / If only + past perfect (had + past participle).
